Help Center & FAQ

An advanced tool designed for engineering AI music prompts. Turn words into musical structures instantly.

1. Enter lyrics or drag a file to auto-distribute.
2. Select your preferred intro and outro.
3. Go to Studio and pick rhythm, mood, and maqam.
4. Generate and copy outputs.

- Enter: Generate prompt.
- Ctrl + S: Save data.
- Ctrl + Z or Ctrl + Y: Undo/Restore.

A smart feature defining your song's conclusion. Choose to fade out, stop abruptly, or end with a solo for a professional touch.

Auto Mode seamlessly switches your interface between Light and Dark themes based on your device's local time (Light from 06:00 to 17:59, and Dark from 18:00 to 05:59).

By using this software, you comply with local copyright laws. Generated outputs are for your use.

All lyrics and prompt data are processed locally in your browser. No data is stored on remote servers.

If you encounter an issue, please contact support at hussien.elmalek@gmail.com.
